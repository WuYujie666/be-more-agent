First-audio experiment (2026-10-21)
Reproduce: venv/bin/python -B experiments/benchmark_first_audio.py
Raw results: experiments/first_audio_results.json
Robot stopped. Real local Ollama and sherpa, no recording or playback.
Two fixed text prompts, four variants, two rounds in reverse order.
Each prompt produced identical full reply text across all eight runs.
Metric: LLM request start to first synthesized audio ready, excluding STT,
VAD, device latency, and later speech chunks. Only first chunk synthesized.

Median seconds, two runs per cell:
                         long first sentence   short first sentence (oh?)
Full sentence LLM4 TTS4       9.918                 2.863
Early clause  LLM4 TTS4       5.176                 3.125
Early clause  LLM3 TTS1       7.839                 4.615
Early clause  LLM2 TTS2       7.249                 3.938

Early clause: split at comma/semicolon/colon after at least eight word
characters, or ordinary sentence ending. Experimental script only.
No reliable benefit for already short first sentences. Reduced threads
were slower on these examples. Prosody, gaps between later chunks, actual
playback, and conversation history were not evaluated. Only two prompts.
Next priority: first-clause playback/prosody evaluation with real short
utterances. Typical STT tests should prioritize complete 2-5 second and
5-10 second speech; the previous 13/25.65-second clips are not representative
of typical brief user turns. Do not infer short-turn latency from them.
